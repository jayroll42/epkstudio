NFC tag & QR code

Export the kit from the Press kit tab and host it (your website, Linktree, Google Drive), then paste its link here.

  1. Buy NTAG215 or NTAG216 stickers or cards. They hold a link, not an audio file.
  2. Install a free tag writer such as NFC Tools on your phone.
  3. Choose Write → Add a record → URL and paste the link: —
  4. Hold the tag to the phone to write it. A tap now opens your kit, where fans hear the snippets.

Wireless MP3 transfer

Save a snippet, then send the MP3 straight to a nearby phone with AirDrop (iPhone, Mac) or Quick Share (Android, Windows). No internet needed on their end.

To send the whole kit in one file, use Hit Me Up.
